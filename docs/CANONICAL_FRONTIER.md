# HNK: AFTERWORLD — Canonical Frontier

**Tehkné Solutions**

Current Assets Forge frontier: **AF-001AT — Water Traversal, Depth & Current Gameplay Runtime**.

AF-001AT turns AF-001AS permanent hydrology into traversal gameplay with depth bands, wading, swimming, current push, stamina costs, exhaustion behavior and crossing-risk assessment.

Temporary streams remain normally traversable; permanent rivers and lakes can become progressively demanding according to depth and current. AF-001AS compatibility remains preserved.

Completion rule remains paired: an AF is complete only after runtime is published in `hnk-rpg-game` and asset/data status is published in `hnk-rpg-game-assets`.
