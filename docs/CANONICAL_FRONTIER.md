Current Assets Forge frontier: **AF-001AS — Persistent Rivers, Lakes & Watershed World System**.

AF-001AS extends AF-001AR temporary runoff into persistent hydrology with springs, creeks, rivers, lakes, groundwater memory, climatic recharge and watershed accumulation.

North Woodland recharges permanent water more strongly; Ring Basin evaporates faster and has shorter groundwater memory. AF-001AR compatibility remains preserved.

Completion rule remains paired: an AF is complete only after runtime is published in `hnk-rpg-game` and asset/data status is published in `hnk-rpg-game-assets`.
